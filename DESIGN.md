## Brand & Style

The brand personality is uncompromising, high-velocity, and precision-engineered. Rooted in elite manufacturing craft and athletic performance, it targets sports team managers, club directors, collegiate athletic departments, and competitive leagues seeking bespoke teamwear. The emotional response evoked must balance industrial power with high-fashion technical sportswear editorial: commanding respect, instant credibility, and elite performance.

The design movement combines **Kinetic Brutalism** and **Technical Editorial**. It departs from generic B2B directories through sharp monolithic geometry, high-contrast typography, structural hairline gridlines, and deep obsidian canvas layers. Visuals mimic luxury performance apparel mockups, tactile technical fabrics, and precise structural schematics rather than generic corporate stock elements.

## Colors

The system is calibrated around a default high-contrast dark environment designed to highlight performance garments like high-end track machinery.

- **Primary Accent (`#ff2a42` - Kinetic Crimson):** Used sparingly for vital conversion triggers, active states, key data callouts, and high-impact micro-accents. It cuts through the dark canvas with urgent athletic energy.
- **Secondary Accent (`#e4e4e7` - Anodized Zinc):** Provides metallic sheen for primary typographic elements, technical badges, and high-contrast structural strokes.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - Base Obsidian: `#0a0a0c`
  - Elevated Plate (Card/Panel): `#141418`
  - Container Intermediary: `#1e1e24`
  - High-Level Layer: `#272730`
- **Muted Zinc (`#a1a1aa`):** Reserved for technical body copy, metric annotations, and deactivated states.
- **Light Mode Transition Rules:** When rendered in light mode, invert to a clean architectural workspace using `#f8f9fa` for ground zero, `#ffffff` for elevated technical cards, `#141418` for dominant typography, and `#d4d4d8` for hairline structural borders while preserving `#ff2a42` for critical action anchors.

## Typography

The typographic system fuses the engineered geometry of **Space Grotesk** with the neutral, hyper-legible utility of **Inter**. 

- **Display & Headings:** Rendered exclusively in Space Grotesk. High-order displays use tight tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.04em`) to construct heavy, kinetic blocks of text reminiscent of technical race apparel and stadium banners.
- **Micro-Labels & Technical Annotations:** All category flags, product SKU trackers, material tags, and quote builder metrics use `label-mono` in full uppercase with aggressive letter tracking (`0.14em`) to establish an athletic manufacturing feel.
- **Body & Editorial Copy:** Inter ensures clean optical scanning across deep technical spec sheets, team bulk pricing charts, and fabric performance breakdowns.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored on an asymmetric 12-column structural grid system built on an 8px architectural cadence.

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` canvas margins. Content panels snap to crisp horizontal and vertical hair lines.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins. Data modules collapse into dual-column sets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen margins. Metric strips convert into continuous horizontal scroll ribbons.
- **Rhythm & Grid Alignment:** Layouts should employ visible division borders rather than arbitrary white space to mimic technical sportswear assembly patterns. Spacing between high-level section transitions relies on `space-xl` to establish an open editorial scale.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects soft diffuse drop shadows and blurred skeuomorphism. Spatial hierarchy is communicated through **structural tonal layering** and **precision hairlines**.

- **Level 0 (Floor):** Pure Canvas (`#0a0a0c`). Serves as the negative space framework.
- **Level 1 (Substrate):** Secondary containers (`#141418`) bordered by a `1px` solid outline in `#272730`. Used for product configurator trays, standard cards, and list blocks.
- **Level 2 (Interactive/Focus):** Elevated plates (`#1e1e24`) utilizing a higher contrast boundary (`#3f3f46`). Applied to active quote steps, drag-and-drop customization zones, and hovering modules.
- **Precision Focus:** Active states do not lift off the page with z-axis shadows; instead, they receive a crisp `1px` inner or outer border highlight in primary crimson (`#ff2a42`) or bright zinc (`#e4e4e7`).

## Shapes

The geometric identity is completely **sharp (0px)**. No rounded corners exist within core functional UI components.

- Every button, input container, modal overlay, image frame, and badge adheres strictly to `roundedness: 0`.
- This absolute rectilinear discipline evokes raw architectural confidence, industrial precision, and fabric cutting patterns. 
- Geometric interest is introduced via chamfered corners (45-degree angle micro-notches) on specialized primary action buttons and badge containers, reinforcing a high-performance aerospace and sports engineering aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Quote Request / Build Kit):** Zero border radius, solid `#ffffff` fill with `#0a0a0c` bold Space Grotesk text in uppercase. Optional 45-degree angled notch on top-right corner. Hover shifts background instantly to `#ff2a42` with text shifting to `#ffffff`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Zero border radius, transparent background, `1px` solid `#3f3f46` border, `#e4e4e7` typography. Hover transitions to `#1e1e24` surface with a `#ffffff` border.
- **Ghost Action:** No background or border. Monospaced tracking text with an accompanying right-angled directional arrow.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background is `#141418` with a `1px` border of `#272730`. Label positioned above in `label-mono` uppercase with muted `#a1a1aa` color.
- Focused state eliminates generic glow rings; it replaces the border with an exact `1px` solid `#ff2a42` perimeter and sharp text cursor.
- Quantity/Team Size Steppers feature sharp modular buttons flanked by monospace numeric readouts.

### Cards (Product & Spec Display)
- Constructed with `#141418` fill and a continuous `1px` border of `#272730`.
- Features an integrated top metadata rail containing SKU code and sport classification formatted with `label-mono`.
- Image presentation utilizes raw architectural aspect ratios (3:4 or 1:1) with subtle desaturation on passive state, springing into high-contrast vivid full-color on hover.

### Badges & Chips
- Rectilinear container with `0.25rem` vertical and `0.5rem` horizontal padding.
- Technical Chips: `#1e1e24` background, `1px` solid `#272730` border, `label-mono` text.
- Status/Performance Badges (e.g., "SUB-ZERO FABRIC", "IN STOCK"): Semi-transparent fill of `#ff2a42` (10% opacity) with a solid `#ff2a42` border and text.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp squares (0px radius) for both checkboxes and radio buttons (radio utilizes an inner square pip instead of a circle).
- Unchecked: `1px` border in `#3f3f46`, background `#0a0a0c`.
- Checked: `#ff2a42` fill with a pure black checkmark or center pip.

### Lists & Technical Data Tables
- Horizontal row separators defined by sharp `1px` lines in `#1e1e24`.
- Alternating column emphasis via monospaced numbers, keeping data headers strictly aligned to the left and numeric specs aligned to the right.

### Team Kit Customizer Ribbon (Specialized Component)
- A floating bottom or lateral strip anchoring total team member roster count, fabric tier selection, and immediate "Request Production Sample" triggers with high-contrast numerical counters.
